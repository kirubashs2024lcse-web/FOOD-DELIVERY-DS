  Evidence: Delay Rate = 44.0%,

Insight 4:

  Evidence: Avg Delivery Time = 39.39 min.

Insight 5:
  Finding: Storm weather is associated with the longest average delivery time.
  Evidence: Avg Delivery Time = 37.88 min.
  Operational meaning: Operational planning should account for adverse weather conditions.

Insight 6:
  Finding: Peak hour orders show a delay rate of 38.0% vs 13.0% for non-peak.
  Evidence: See peak_hour_analysis.csv in output/results.
  Operational meaning: Staffing and rider pre-positioning during peak hours can reduce delays.

Insight 7:
  Finding: Distance correlation with Delivery_Time is 0.631 — moderate, not dominant.
  Evidence: Random Forest top feature = 'Distance_KM', suggesting other factors matter more.
  Operational meaning: Distance alone does not explain delays; traffic, prep time, and area
                       are equally or more important predictive factors.
